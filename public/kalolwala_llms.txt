Kalolwala & Associates

  Kalolwala & Associates is an India-based corporate communication and
  stakeholder advisory firm specializing in annual reports, integrated
  reports, ESG and sustainability reporting, investor communications,
  corporate branding, corporate websites, and digital communication
  solutions. The firm helps publicly listed companies, large
  enterprises, and organizations communicate effectively with
  stakeholders, investors, and regulators through strategic reporting,
  branding, and digital experiences.
